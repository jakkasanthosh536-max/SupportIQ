# NovaStore Polar Expeditions & Remote Arctic Shipping Policy

## Overview
NovaStore now supports specialized fulfillment for polar research expeditions, scientific research stations, and remote military postal addresses (APO/FPO/DPO).

## Delivery Methods & Timelines
1. **Chartered Polar Airfreight**: Delivered via seasonal transport flights to McMurdo Station, South Pole Station, and certified Arctic research outposts.
2. **Transit Windows**: Due to extreme sub-zero weather conditions, shipments depart twice monthly during operational research seasons (October through March for Antarctic bases).
3. **Packaging Requirements**: All polar consignments are hermetically sealed in multi-layer insulated thermal moisture barriers to protect electronic equipment from freezing damage.
4. **Custom Inquiries**: Contact polar-logistics@novastore.com with station coordinates and customs manifests for expedited routing.
