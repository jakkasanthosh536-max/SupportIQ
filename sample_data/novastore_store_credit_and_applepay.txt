NovaStore Billing FAQ: Split Payments with Store Credit and Apple Pay

Q: Can I use both my NovaStore Store Credit and Apple Pay for a single purchase?
A: Yes! Our checkout system fully supports split-tender transactions.

How it works:
1. When checking out on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac Safari browser, proceed to the Payment Step.
2. Under "Apply Credits", check the box next to "Use Available Store Credit ($XX.XX)".
3. The order total will instantly update to show the remaining unpaid balance.
4. Select "Apple Pay" for the remaining balance and authenticate using Touch ID or Face ID.
5. You will receive a unified invoice displaying the split payment attribution.
